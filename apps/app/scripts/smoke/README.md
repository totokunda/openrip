# App smoke harness

Drives the built app (`npm run build` in `apps/app`) with Playwright on a throwaway profile:

```sh
node session.mjs <appDir> <outDir> <userDataDir> <clip> <step...>
# e.g. the standard clip:
node session.mjs ../.. out/std out/ud-std /path/to/IMG_9019.MOV import timeline play perf
# a CapCut draft (the card named CAPCUT_DRAFT, else the first whose text contains it):
CAPCUT_DRAFT=… node session.mjs ../.. out/cc out/ud-cc x capcut perf
```

- Use a fresh `userDataDir` per run; `out/` is ignored by git. A step can quit and start the app again on the same profile with `await ctx.relaunch()` (it returns the new `{ app, win }`, and `ctx.app` / `ctx.win` follow; the app opens on its home screen).
- The app opens on its home screen; the session opens the project it had open last (a fresh profile's Project 1) before the first step, so steps start in the editor. `STAY_HOME=1` starts on the home screen.
- `OPENRIP_TRACE=1` logs the engine's sync, frame and decode timings to `logs.txt`; `OPENRIP_ENGINE_MUTE=1` silences playback.
- `EXE=<binary>` runs a packaged build.
- Screenshots are at 2x: CSS coordinates are half their pixels. (`seek` alone still takes coordinates, SEEK_X and RULER_Y, for poking at a layout by hand.)
- Steps: `home` (back to the home screen: the projects listed, the open one marked, its cover, the new-project sizes from the Custom size card; then into the editor again), `shell` (after `import timeline newtext`: every left-panel tab, the inspector's tabs for a text and a video, a text style from the left panel and the Transitions tab on a clip with no cut, the safe-area toggle, the project's details, the ratio menu, the four layouts from the layout menu and a dragged panel's size saved, with a shot of each), `panels` (the panels' sizes; `PANEL_ASSETS_WIDTH` checks the left panel's after a restart on the same profile), `import`, `timeline`, `play`, `perf` (seek, scrub, playback, edit latency), `profstart`/`profstop` (renderer CPU profile), `capcut`, `capfolder` (a draft folder anywhere, `CAPCUT_FOLDER`), `seek`, `selcaption`, `capstyle`, `capanim`, `capbox` (a caption's box after a case and font change), `capadjust` (the draft's own adjustments in the Adjust tab), `capcolour` (clarity, vignette, fade, an HSL range and curves in the Adjust tab, on a clip that has them all), `capsticker` (a sticker drawn, and its opacity in the Filter tab), `capfilter` (a CapCut filter bar's intensity). Helpers for new steps are in `steps/_preview.mjs`.
- Canvas tools: `canvastools` (after `import timeline`): the player's header holds only its name and menu (no tool row); the zoom popover; Stickers ▸ Shapes adds a star at the playhead; Stickers ▸ Draw's pen draws a clip, with the tool chip and Esc; T and V; the Mask tab's picker starts a rectangle mask dragged on the video.
- The player: `player` (after `import timeline`; run it on `~/Downloads/IMG_9079.MOV` with `CLIP_NAME=IMG_9079 OPENRIP_ENGINE_MUTE=1`, a 4K ProRes clip with sound): the bottom bar's order (time, meter; play; safe areas, quality, zoom, ratio, full screen); the header menu's three items and its submenus; the colour oscilloscope (RGB parade, waveform, vectorscope) drawn from the engine's frame, its waveform's level against the frame's, changing with it, and playback's frame rate with it on; ⌘⌥R rulers, a guide dragged out of the top ruler, the video snapping onto it, saved, ⌘Z / ⇧⌘Z, ⌘;, the guide's menu and Edit guide, Add vertical guide, Clear guides; Export still frames (the save dialog stubbed to `out/…/still.png`, its size and its pixels against the engine's frame); the zoom popover (+, −, the slider, the navigator, ⌥⇧Z); each quality's playback render size; the meter lit while playing and grey paused; the safe-area toggle; ⌘⇧F full screen and Esc. Shots are named after the CapCut references (`p09-color-oscilloscope` ↔ `16-player/009-color-oscilloscope.png`).
- Keyframes: `keyframes` (after `import timeline`): keys set through the store with `lib/keyframes.ts` on the clip (opacity and position), the engine's frames checked over time, CapCut's edit-at-the-playhead rule, a split, and the saved project; `keyframescheck` (after `reopen`): the keys come back from the file and the engine draws them. Both import the renderer's modules, so they need its dev server: `npx vite --port 5199` in `packages/renderer`, and the session run with `MODE=development VITE_DEV_SERVER_URL=http://localhost:5199` (and `CLIP_NAME` for a clip other than IMG_9019).
- Transitions and clip animations: `transitions` (after `import timeline`, with the dev server as for `keyframes`): set through the store with `lib/animations.ts` on the clip split in two (its right half darkened): a Mix's frames across its window, a Push and a Wipe at the cut, Fade In and Fade Out, a split keeping the In on its left part with the frames unchanged, a Swing combo spanning its part, and the saved project.
- Speed tools: `speed` (after `import timeline`, with the dev server as for `keyframes`): through the store with `lib/speed.ts`, Reverse (1 s in shows what 1 s before the end did, it plays in real time, ⌘Z), Flash In's curve (the clip's length, a frame at the media the remap gives, ⌘Z), and Freeze at 2 s (a 3 s still of that frame, the rest 3 s later, saved); then an export, silent through the still, its sound after it the clip's from 2 s to within 12 ms.
- Colour and compositing: `compositing` (after `import timeline`, with the dev server as for `keyframes`; `CLIP_NAME` for the clip): set through the store with lib/color.ts, lib/compositing.ts and lib/transform-presets.ts on the first video or photo: the clip at half size with a red canvas fill, then a blur of itself, then an image (`CANVAS_IMAGE`); Darken and Screen against the red canvas; a mirror (the frame mirrored); exposure, HSL and a .cube LUT; a chroma key on the clip's middle colour and its intensity keyframed; the old contrast slider moved onto CapCut's; the saved project.
- Text styles and captions: `textstyles` (after `import timeline`, with the dev server as for `keyframes`): a text from `lib/text-presets.ts` through the store, then Character and Line spacing (the text widens and its lines spread by CapCut's 1/20 em a step), a gradient fill, a second outline, a glow and its keyframes, an Effects style from `lib/text-styles.ts` put on and taken off, the saved fields; then `fixtures/captions.srt` read with `lib/captions.ts` onto a captions track (timing, the overlap ended at the next cue, each caption drawn at its time) and the track written back out as SRT (`out/…/captions-out.srt`). `captextstyle` (after `capcut`, same dev server): a converted caption's spacing shown in CapCut's panel units, Character and a glow changing the engine's frame, and the draft's frame back when the fields are put back.
- Audio tools: `audio` (after `import timeline` on a clip with sound, e.g. `~/Downloads/IMG_9079.MOV` with `CLIP_NAME=IMG_9079`; the dev server as for `keyframes`; run with `OPENRIP_FAKE_MIC=1 OPENRIP_ENGINE_MUTE=1`): through the store with lib/audio.ts and lib/audio-effects.ts, each checked in an export: Normalize loudness (the clip at the level the gain predicts, by FFmpeg's ebur128), Fill channel left (both channels the same), Telephone (lows and highs cut), strength 0 (the clip unchanged); then a voiceover from the engine's stand-in microphone (a 440 Hz tone; nothing real is recorded): its level while recording, the file in the project's media and library, placed with `placeVoiceover`, its tone in an export.
- Text panels, by their controls in the built app (no dev server):
  - `texttab` (after `import timeline newtext`): every control of Text ▸ Basic (the text box, Font, Font size, B U I, Case, Color through the picker's Hex field, Character and Line spacing, Alignment, Stroke, Background, Glow, Shadow, the gradient fill) and an Effects style and None, each changing the engine's frame and the clip as saved; the text, font, bold, colour, stroke, glow intensity and style undone and redone (`undoCase`). `texttabcheck` (after `reopen`): the clip's 33 fields back from the file, and the engine's same frame.
  - `textpresets` (after `import timeline`): the Text tab's column; the default text tile adds a text at the playhead (undone and redone), selected on its Text tab; a category and the search filter the presets; a preset clicked, and one dragged onto the timeline, are added at the playhead.
  - `captionsio` (after `import timeline`, nothing else on the timeline): `fixtures/captions.srt` imported in the Yellow style (the file dialog stubbed) onto its own track with the file's timing, drawn, undone and redone as one step; its card; a template on the selected caption's whole track; Export captions (the save dialog stubbed) writing the same SRT to `out/…/captions-export.srt`.
  - `fontcheck` (after `import timeline newtext`): a word in each bundled font (and Poppins Bold and three installed ones) picked in the font menu; the engine's glyphs (its frame with the word white minus black) against the browser's canvas in the same font, and in Arial: IoU of the shapes. `FONTS="A,B"` checks some only.
  - Helpers for Text ▸ Basic's controls (`textCase`, `textFont`, `textColor`) are in `steps/_undo.mjs`.
- Settings and shortcuts: `settings` (on a fresh profile; `STAY_HOME=1` or not): the home gear's menu (Settings, Third-party copyright with OpenRip's npm and Cargo notices, Version opening About), the menu bar's app menu (About, Settings, Back to home page, Layout ▸, More ▸ Third-party Copyright, Quit) and its windows; every tab of the Settings window and its rows (disabled ones with a note, no cloud rows), the menus' choices; Cancel leaving everything as it was; then saved changes acting: a new project at Frame rate 29.97, the inspector's Modify, a photo (made with ffmpeg) dropped at Image duration 40 s, the player's time in HH:MM:SS:FF and HH:MM:SS+frame, the cache counted and cleared, ⇧→ / ⇧← stepping 5 frames, ⇧↑ in a value field adding 5 steps, Save to moving the projects; a restart on the same profile (`ctx.relaunch()`) keeping it all and deleting cache files older than the age chosen; the projects moved back. `shortcuts` (after `import timeline`): the Shortcuts window from Help ▸ Shortcuts and the top bar's keyboard, each tab's rows and keys, the presets; Split moved to ⌘K (the field, Save turning on), Q refused as Delete left's; ⌘K splitting and ⌘B not; Reset. Helpers in `steps/_settings.mjs` (preferences, menus, the menu bar, a photo, imports).
- Undo: `undo` (after `import timeline newtext`: each kind of edit undone with ⌘Z and redone with ⇧⌘Z, the engine's frame and the saved project compared; then everything undone and redone by the toolbar), `capundo` (the same on a draft: a caption, a video, the timeline; `DRAFT_ADJUST` for the draft's own adjustments), `undoreopen` (after `reopen`: nothing left to undo). Helpers in `steps/_undo.mjs`.
- The player shows time as HH:MM:SS:FF; `clock()` and `now()` read its element's seconds (`data-time`, `data-total`) and logs keep `MM:SS.ss/MM:SS.ss`.
- Controls are found by `data-testid` (`media-import`, `tool-split`, `play`, `ratio-menu`, `go-home`, `project-card`, `assets-tab-<tab>`, …), by role and by text; the inspector's tabs by name inside `[data-testid=inspector]` (`tab()` in `steps/_preview.mjs`; "Transform" and "Mask" find their sub-tab). Clips are found in the project as saved (the first caption, the first filter bar, the first clip with the draft's adjustments, …) and clicked on their bar from the ruler's mapping (`clipsWhere`, `focus`, `select`, `rowOf` in `steps/_anim.mjs`), so no step needs coordinates measured from a screenshot. Clicking the ruler seeks; it also drops the selection, and a click on the playhead's line grabs the playhead, not the clip under it (6 px either side of it). `seek` (`_preview.mjs`) clicks the ruler once and steps the rest a frame at a time with ← / →, so it lands on the frame asked for.
- Steps print `PASS`/`FAIL` lines where they check something (`checks` in `steps/_anim.mjs`, with the Animation tab's helpers, the project as saved and where a clip's bar is).

## Timeline editing

`node tl-suite.mjs ../.. /path/to/clip [case...]` runs each case on a fresh profile under `out/tl/` (`CLIP_NAME` when the clip isn't IMG_9019; `TL_DRAFT` for the CapCut case). Every edit is undone and redone with the engine's frame and the saved project compared (`undoCase`); helpers in `steps/_timeline.mjs`.

- `tlmagnet` (after `import timeline`): ⌘B splits the main track's clip; ripple delete; the magnet toggled off (a delete keeps its gap) and on; Q and W; a clip dragged to the front of the main track goes in there; one dragged off it leaves it closed up.
- `tllink`: ⇧⌘S detaches the audio, linked (and the engine still draws the video); ⌘B cuts both; a trim, a move (magnet off) and a delete take the audio along; with linkage off (`) the video moves alone; Unlink.
- `tlsnap` (after `newtext`): the text's start snaps to the playhead (guide line drawn; not with N off), its end to a marker and to the video's end, a trimmed edge to the playhead.
- `tlmarkers`: M twice, tags under the ruler, ⌥← / ⌥→, the box's colour, name and time, a tag dragged (snapping), Delete, Delete all markers in timeline.
- `tltracks` (after `newtext`): + ▸ Audio track (it stays empty through other edits), lock (the clip can't be selected, deleted, moved or trimmed), a header dragged to the top, a video track can't go under the main track, Delete track (from the "…" menu, then a right-click, with its clip).
- `tlhandle` (after `newtext`): pressing a clip's trim handles changes nothing, and ⌘Z still undoes the edit before.
- `tlkeys` (after `newtext`): ← / → and ⇧→, Home / End, ↓ / ↑ between edits, Space, ⌘= (the timeline zooms, not the page) and ⇧Z, the playhead's handle let go staying on its frame at every zoom, ] and ⌘A.
- `tlcapcut` (after `capcut`): the draft's magnet from its config; ripple delete, Q, an insert and a detach on converted clips, drawn by the engine.

## Motion and timing

`node motion-suite.mjs ../.. /path/to/clip [case...]` runs each case on a fresh profile under `out/motion/` (`CLIP_NAME` when the clip isn't IMG_9019; `MOTION_DEV` the renderer's dev server URL for `keys`, skipped without it). Helpers for transitions on cuts are in `steps/_anim.mjs` (`addTransition`, `pickTransition`, `transitionName`, `transitionsOffered`, `deleteTransition`); `CHECKS_LIVE=1` prints each check as it's made.

- `transui` (case `trans`, after `import timeline`): the Transitions tab's column and cards, + with a clip selected, the transition's inspector (name, duration, at most the shorter clip), its marker clicked and its side dragged, a card dragged onto a cut, the direction, right-click ▸ Delete and ⌫, + on the last clip, Apply to all; the engine's frames, ⌘Z / ⇧⌘Z.
- `animclip` (case `clipanim`): a video's Animation tab: In, Out and Combo cards, durations bounded by the clip (the Out by what the In leaves), a Combo replacing the In and Out and back, None; the engine's frames.
- `speedui` (case `speed`): Standard (2× in the field, 2 s in showing 4 s, Duration, Change audio pitch, Reset, the ends of the scale) and Curve (the presets, Flash In, the editor's points dragged, added and removed at the playhead, Reset, None).
- `freezeui` (case `freeze`): the toolbar's transform tools (Freeze at the playhead on the main track, off on a still; Reverse and back; Mirror) and the clip menu's Edit › Reverse and Freeze.
- `keysui` (case `keys`, dev server): diamonds on the selected clip's bar at its keys, a click to the playhead, a drag moving the keys, the right-click menu's ease and Delete keyframe.

## Animation tab

`node anim-suite.mjs ../.. /path/to/IMG_9019.MOV [case...]` runs every case below, each on a fresh profile under `out/anim/`, and prints the checks. The CapCut cases take their drafts from `DRAFT_*` variables (what each needs is in the suite's header) and are skipped without them.

- The tab is CapCut's: sub-tabs In / Out / Combo (Loop on a text) of cards; `choose`, `selected` and `options` in `steps/_anim.mjs` take the sub-tab ("Animation" is In) or "Words" (the caption style's select). Transitions are on cuts: the Transitions tab's + or a card dragged, the marker on the cut for its inspector.
- The app's own clips: `newtext` (a text 0–3 s over the standard clip), `animtext` (each typing In hides the text at its start, shows part of it, then all; duration; None; Fade Out as the Out, Pulse as the Loop), `textcut` (a trim shorter than the animation, a split), `animvideo` (the clip split in two; White Flash, White Fade, duration, ⌫ on cuts; + on the last clip goes on the cut before it; no Blink; a split keeps the transition at its cut), `animundo`.
- Saving: `animsetup` (Type 1 and Fade Out on the text, White Flash at 5 s), `persist` and `persistcheck` (the tab's values, the transitions' inspector and frames before and after `reopen`, which goes home, makes a new project and opens this one again from its card, or after a restart), `quickset`/`quickcheck` (a change with nothing after it reaches the project file), `ticks` (a draft's clips keep the draft's timing).
- Drafts: `capin` (In animations shown, edited, replaced), `capkeep` (one the engine doesn't draw), `capwords` and `capwords2` (caption styles, an In animation on a caption, trims; a style the engine doesn't draw), `captrans`, `capblink`, `blinkapp` (Blink copied into the app's own project), `animtabs` (which clips get the tab), `capedit` (edits for `persist`), `capinfo` (writes the opened project's tracks and clips to `capinfo.txt`, for writing steps).
- `animexport`: an export through the engine, its frames checked with ffmpeg.
- `menuundo` (after `capcut selcaption`): Edit ▸ Undo and Redo chosen from the menu bar undo and redo the editor's edits.
