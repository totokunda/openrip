Footage on the Transitions and Effects tiles
=============================================

Three short clips (street.mp4, forest.mp4, beach.mp4), each cut to 3 seconds, scaled to 360 x 202,
and re-encoded without sound from videos on Wikimedia Commons. All three are dedicated to the
public domain by their authors under CC0 1.0 Universal
(https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/); no credit is required, and it is given here
anyway.

street.mp4  (from 11.5 s)
  "View of Braga Street in Bandung 2, 13 June 2024", by Jeromi Mikhael
  https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:View_of_Braga_Street_in_Bandung_2,_13_June_2024.webm

forest.mp4  (from 6 s)
  "Golden hour rays break through mist on Diana Creek", by Extemporalist
  https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Golden_hour_rays_break_through_mist_on_Diana_Creek.webm

beach.mp4  (from 14.2 s)
  "Body surfing a wave", by JaredMcKenzie
  https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Body_surfing_a_wave.webm

The engine draws each transition and effect on these clips when a tile is first shown
(apps/app/packages/main/src/modules/TilePreviews.ts) and keeps the result in the rendering cache.
