# CapCut doesn't kern (2026-10-16)

A highlighted caption word in CapCut's export of draft A came out 3–7% wider than ours with the same glyph heights; the gaps in pairs like "WA" and "AT" were wider. Shaping the words in the caption font (ZY Resolve) with and without its GPOS kerning gives exactly that: "WATCHING" is 7.0% wider unkerned, and CapCut's was 7.3% wider than ours. CapCut's text engine lays text out on the glyphs' plain advances, so CapCut text is now shaped with kerning off. The app's own text keeps it (a browser canvas kerns).

Against the glyph-placement fix of the same day (results/2026-10-16-glyph-offset.md), same references:

The other exports don't change (2, 6, 11) or are of a different edit or missing media.

**Draft covers** (`cover-parity`, 31 drafts): the median goes from 29.0 to 32.6 dB; five covers improve (+2.3, +5.1, +7.9, +9.3, +14.4 dB) and none get worse.

CapCut's wrap width per unit (text-wrap.md) was fitted with kerned widths; with these results it still holds on the exports above, but a refit on the wrapping calibration would confirm it.
