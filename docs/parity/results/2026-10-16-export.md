# Export — 2026-10-16

`openrip-dev export <draft or project> <out.mp4>`. Apple M2 Max.
- **Pipeline:** each frame is composited on the GPU straight into an NV12 IOSurface (BT.709, limited range), then encoded by VideoToolbox H.264 through FFmpeg as a hardware frame. There are no pixel copies and no CPU colour conversion.
- **Scope:** video only so far; audio comes with the mixer.

## Same draft, OpenRip's export vs CapCut's export

Calibration session 2's draft, 1080p30 H.264, both files decoded the same way (BT.709 limited range):

| Frame | PSNR | Pixels off by > 64/255 |
|---|---|---|
| Pattern 100% | **57.2 dB** | 0.00% |
| Pattern 50% | **63.5 dB** | 0.00% |
| Pattern 50%, rotated 45° | **42.3 dB** | 0.02% |
| Default text, size 15 / 30 / 10 | 38.1 / 29.8 / 30.6 dB | 0.09 / 0.41 / 0.18% |

Container match: H.264 High, yuv420p, `tv` range, BT.709 primaries, transfer and matrix, as in CapCut's file. The remaining text gaps are the ones in [calibration.md](../calibration.md): vertical pixel snapping and weight.

## Speed

| Draft | Output | Time | Speed |
|---|---|---|---|
| Calibration session 2 (stills and text, 6 s) | 1920×1080 @ 30 | 0.74 s | **8.1× real time** |
| A real phone draft (4K H.264 portrait source, 63 s) | 1080×1920 @ 24 | 10.6 s (render 10.0, encode 0.4) | **6.0× real time** |

Rendering, decode included, is almost all of the time. Decoding ahead on another thread while compositing is the next step.
