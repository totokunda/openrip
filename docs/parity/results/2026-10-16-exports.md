# OpenRip against CapCut's own exports — 2026-10-16

Ten local drafts have a CapCut export whose length matches the draft (CapCut's export folder: `<name>/<name>.mp4`). `openrip-dev export-parity <draft> <export.mp4>` renders the draft at the export's resolution every 2 s. It brings both frames to half the canvas size with the same area filter and compares them. Drafts are numbered, not named. Two of the ten have most of their media missing and aren't counted.

PSNR p50 per draft. "First run" is today's first measurement; the changes between the two runs are listed below.

| Draft | First run | Now | What still differs |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 22.3 dB | **31.7 dB** | |
| 2 | 16.8 dB | **31.6 dB** | the filters' AI skin mask isn't reproduced |
| 3 | 29.2 dB | 29.0 dB | the draft was edited after the export |
| 4 | 27.4 dB | 28.5 dB | the title's paper cut-out text style is drawn now (+1.5 to +2.8 dB on those frames; the median doesn't move) |
| 5 | 21.5 dB | **27.8 dB** | a library text shape behind captions |
| 6 | 24.2 dB | 24.8 dB | CapCut exported at 480×836: edge softness only |
| 7 | 23.1 dB | 23.3 dB | curved text, text effects, subtitle templates |
| 8 | 20.9 dB | 21.0 dB | subtitle templates (word-by-word highlight) not drawn |

**What moved the numbers:**
- **Colour.** CapCut's Adjust sliders, primary colour wheels, Sharpen and filter tracks, reimplemented from the colour bundle CapCut downloads ([adjust.md](../adjust.md)).
- **Render size.** The export is now rendered at the export's own resolution, as CapCut renders it.

**Checked and found right:**
- **Frame timing.** We show the same source frame CapCut does. A one-frame "offset" seen at first was a probe that rendered 20 ms into the frame.
- **Text wrapping.** It matches on every caption without a library text effect ([text-wrap.md](../text-wrap.md)).

**Found and fixed along the way:** layers zoomed past the GPU's 8,192 px texture limit crashed the compositor (wgpu panic). Rendering one draft at its 1440×2560 export size hit it.
