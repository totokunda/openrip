## How to fill in the CapCut column

1. Open CapCut, create a new project, import the 60 s clip, put it on the timeline, set preview quality to Full, and leave other settings at their defaults.
2. Run:
   `node bench/sample-procs.mjs --match "CapCut" --decoder --delay 5 --seconds 15 --label "capcut-9.5.0 4K60 HEVC" --out docs/parity/results/2026-10-15-capcut-4k60.json`
3. Within 5 seconds, switch to CapCut and press play. Leave it playing until the command prints its summary.
